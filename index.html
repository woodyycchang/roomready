If you sign an unfurnished lease from another country, day one starts in an empty room.

You pick furniture for a room you’ve only seen in listing photos. Orders show up on different days, sometimes before you’ve even landed. And after a long flight, nobody wants to put a bed frame together.

How it works

Most of it happens while you’re still packing.

  1. I.

    Tell us about your place

    Share the listing or floor plan, your move-in date, and a few photos of rooms you like. Screenshots from Pinterest, Instagram, or Xiaohongshu are fine.

  2. II.

    See the plan

    On a video call we walk you through a floor plan and every item, with photos and prices. We don’t buy anything until you approve it. You pay half at that point.

  3. III.

    We set it up

    The furniture ships to your apartment and a local crew puts it together and arranges the room. You pay the second half before they start. We send you photos when the room is done.

  4. IV.

    Walk in

    Pick up your keys and open the door. We’ll check in during your first week in case anything needs fixing.

Questions we’ve heard so far.

How do you get into my apartment before I arrive?

That depends on your landlord. We’d follow whatever key process they use, and you’d see photos of the finished room before your flight.

What if I don’t like something?

You approve each item before we buy it. If something sells out, we check with you before picking a replacement.

Who pays, me or my parents?

Whoever you like. You plan it with us and either of you can pay. The price is fixed before we buy anything.

Is RoomReady open yet?

No. We’re five Cornell students, and we’re still working out the details by talking to students who are moving.

Which cities will you start in?

A few college towns first. If you tell us where you’re moving, it helps us decide.

What happens after I join the list?

We’ll email you when RoomReady is ready. Before then, one of us may write to ask how your move went. We won’t share your email with anyone.
